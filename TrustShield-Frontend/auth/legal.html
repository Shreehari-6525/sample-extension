Terms of Service

TrustShield AI is currently a UI prototype. By using it you acknowledge that:

  • Scan results, scores and reports shown are demonstration data, not real threat detection.

Privacy Policy

In this prototype build:

  • Preferences, scan history and the demo session are stored only in your browser's local extension storage.
  • No data is sent to any server, and no Google credentials are requested or stored.
  • You can remove all stored data by signing out and removing the extension.

The production privacy policy will describe the data handled by the TrustShield backend.
